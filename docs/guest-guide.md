# A quick check before your Temporal webinar

Open the pre-flight invite supplied by your webinar organizer on the same laptop, browser and network you plan to use during the event. Keep your usual company VPN connected if your organization requires it.

After the sandbox is created, everything starts automatically. Confirm that you can see Ziggy, the code editor and Temporal UI. Open the Terminal tab, look for **ZIGGY ONLINE** and a ticking clock, then return to Launchpad to confirm it. Click **Check** to finish. You do not need to install anything, type commands, edit files or provide an API key.

If a preview is too small, use its full-size tab. Choose the issue response for anything that stays blank, blocked or frozen. Launchpad provides a short readiness report you can review and copy to your organizer.

## If you cannot open the invite or Launchpad

Send this to your webinar organizer using the event’s normal contact channel:

> I could not complete the Temporal pre-flight.
> The failing page/tab is: ____.
> My browser is: ____.
> I am using a managed work laptop: yes / no.
> I am on a corporate network or required VPN: yes / no.
> The time and time zone were: ____.
> The visible error says: ____.

Do not include passwords, API keys or unrelated company information. Ask IT to review [Instruqt’s network requirements](https://docs.instruqt.com/reference/platform/third-party-network-access), including HTTPS and secure WebSockets to the sandbox domains on port 443.

Use a current, organization-approved browser. Keep company security controls enabled. Your IT team or organizer can help arrange an approved alternative setup.

A successful pre-flight means you could see the prepared tools at that time. The webinar will exercise typing, editing and the workshop itself.
